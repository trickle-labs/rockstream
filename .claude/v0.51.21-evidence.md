## Wiring audit

No SQL or wire surface was added, so pgwire reachability and negative `RS-XXXX`
tests are not applicable. Equivalent connector wiring is present and exercised:

- `kafka_source.rs` imports and constructs `StreamConsumer`; `KafkaSource::connect`
  is called by `kafka_source_exactly_once_tests.rs`, and source polling commits
  through the real consumer.
- `kafka_sink.rs` constructs the real producer, calls
  `begin_transaction`/`send_offsets_to_transaction`/`commit_transaction`, and
  `KafkaSink::connect` is called by the real-broker proof.
- `object_store_sink.rs` uses `ObjectStore` with `PutMode::Create`; both real
  MinIO tests call `ObjectStoreSink::new` with a bucket client.
- The only non-real test caller uses `object_store::memory::InMemory` explicitly
  as a bounded unit-test backend; no production fallback was added.
