The 4-stage pipeline is fully wired:
1. **Parser / Lowering**: `rockstream-sql/src/lower.rs` lowers `SUM`, `COUNT`, `AVG`, `MIN`, `MAX` to `AggregateExpr`.
2. **Gate / Rejection**: `rockstream-sql/src/compile.rs` validates operator memory requirements and rejects unsupported operators before allocation.
