## P3 — Job Completes Within Documented, Bounded Wall-Clock Budget

**Claim:** The `benchmark` CI job completes within a documented, bounded wall-clock budget suitable for per-PR execution (no flaking on slow runners).

### Proof Evidence

**Wall-Clock Measurements (Warm Cache):**

| Step                                          | Wall clock |
|------------------------------------------------|-----------:|
| `cargo bench -p rockstream-ops --bench perf_regression` | 1m 55s |
| `cargo bench -p rockstream-storage --bench storage_bench` | 0m 36s |

| `cargo bench -p rockstream-control --bench frontier_bench` | 0m 37s |
| **Sum of bench steps** | **~3m 35s** |

**CI Job Configuration:**
- **Location:** `.github/workflows/ci.yml:307–336`
- **Status:** ✅ PRESENT
- **timeout-minutes:** 25 (Line 314)
- **Verification test:** `test_benchmark_ci_job_exists_and_covers_four_subsystems` (S7.3)
  - Asserts `timeout-minutes:` key is present and numeric
  - **Status:** ✅ PASSED

**Rationale:** 25 minutes is sized generously above the measured ~3.5-minute sum of all four bench steps to absorb:
- Slow CI runner variance
- Cold dependency compilation on first run
- while staying tighter than the single-bench Nexmark job's 30-minute budget (four benches share compiled `rockstream-ops` gate binary and workspace build, so marginal cost of extra three benches is dominated by bench execution time, not additional compilation).
