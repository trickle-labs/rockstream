## 5. Admission gate

Add `scripts/check-connector-admission.sh` and
`scripts/check-connector-admission.test.sh`, following the root-directory and
exact-output convention of `check-no-mock-connectors.sh` and its self-test.
The checker derives a candidate connector from a public module in
`crates/rockstream-connectors/src/lib.rs` that implements `SourceConnector` or
`SinkConnector`. The retained `PostgresCdcSource`, `KafkaSource`, and
`KafkaSink` are the fixed baseline. Every additional candidate requires a
non-empty admission record in `docs/connectors.md` with these six exact keys:

1. `core_ivm_improvement`
2. `no_kafka_or_postgres_boundary`
3. `demonstrated_production_demand`
4. `failure_and_recovery_semantics`
5. `acceptable_maintenance_burden`
6. `permanent_compatibility_value`

The script rejects missing, duplicate, blank, or placeholder (`TBD`, `N/A`,
`TODO`) fields. Its self-test first adds a synthetic public sink module and
asserts the complete failure output; it then adds all six populated fields and
asserts the complete success output. This proves both directions without
requiring a test-only connector dependency or an uncompiled production module.

Wire both scripts into `.github/workflows/ci.yml` beside the existing
no-mock-connector gate. Do not add a new CI workflow or dependency.
