# v0.58.1 Plan — Real-Backend Failure Proof & Published Recovery Numbers

## Scope and Boundary

v0.58.1 implements the real-process, real-backend counterparts for the production failure matrix in `real-cluster-chaos.yml` and publishes absolute detection, recovery, and freshness numbers against the v0.22 and v0.31 SLOs (per `ROCKSTREAM_PROJECT_FOCUS.md` §5.5 and `NEW_ROADMAP.md` v0.58.1).

Per the 2026-08-12 realism review split:
- **v0.58**: Published failure matrix in `docs/failure-matrix.md`, programmatic registry in `crates/rockstream-sim/src/failure_matrix.rs`, deterministic `SimRuntime` coverage across all 11 production failure modes with permanent seed corpus, non-vacuous assertion CI gate (`scripts/check-failure-matrix.sh`).
- **v0.58.1 (this version)**: Every cell of the v0.58 matrix whose failure mode is observable against a real system gets a real-process, real-backend counterpart in the `real-cluster-chaos.yml` workflow (and `crates/rockstream-sim/tests/real_cluster_chaos_soak_tests.rs`):
  1. Object-store brownout and throttling (FM-005)
  2. Source disconnect with offset/LSN recovery (FM-004)
  3. Sink failure during commit **and** during recovery (FM-008)
  4. Control-node loss (FM-002)
  5. Exchange interruption and retry-budget exhaustion (FM-003)
  6. Resource exhaustion with recovery (FM-011)
  7. Worker loss during active epoch / shard processing (FM-001)
  All remaining cells have explicit real-backend counterparts or reasoned exemptions recorded in `docs/failure-matrix.md` and `failure_matrix.rs`.
  Publishes **absolute** detection, recovery, and freshness numbers against the v0.22 and v0.31 SLOs (rather than a relative regression check) as a checked-in artifact (`docs/chaos-recovery-baseline.json`).
  Enforces a documented schedule with a per-cell time budget and published total runtime.
  Verifies that an injected recovery delay past the SLO fails the gate rather than being absorbed as noise.
- **v0.58.2**: Storage-pressure admission signals and the SLO auto-tuning lock.
- **v0.58.3**: Unscoped reachability, dispatch-wiring & silent-wrong-answer sweep.

### Non-Negotiable Ground Rules
- `rockstream` remains the sole production binary and `main` remains runnable.
- Prefix all shell commands with `rtk` (e.g. `rtk cargo test`, `rtk git status`).
- No code path may depend on SlateDB range deletion; cleanup is scan-and-delete or snapshot-safe compaction filters.
- Unbounded in-memory accumulation is prohibited: every buffer/queue/window has a named upper bound, fill metric, and backpressure/error path.
- No test may be weakened, skipped (`#[ignore]`), or deleted.
- **Nothing may be deferred to a later version.**
