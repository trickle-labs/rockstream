## Vertical test-first slices

### S1 — Local non-poisoning primitives

- **Owned files:** `gateway/src/auth.rs`, `control/src/namespace.rs`, `control/src/acl.rs`, `sql/src/frontend.rs`, their focused tests, and affected crate manifests.
- **Failing tests first:** the four local state matrix tests above; each panics while a test-only critical-section hook holds the lock, then verifies the exact peer result.
- **Implementation:** add the minimal test-only injection hooks, migrate to `parking_lot`, remove `LockResult`/`PoisonError` recovery branches, and retain short lock scope before any await.
- **Proof:** OIDC, namespace, ACL, and SQL snapshot state cannot cascade a holder panic into a peer panic, error, or stall.

### S2 — Gateway webhook and process-wide DLQ containment

- **Owned files:** `gateway/src/server.rs`, `types/src/dlq.rs`, gateway/types manifests, and `crates/rockstream-gateway/tests/lock_poisoning_containment_tests.rs` (or focused unit modules where privacy requires).
- **Failing tests first:** the webhook, DLQ, and real pgwire peer-connection matrix tests; each asserts exact accepted/result frames and retained availability after the injected holder panic.
- **Implementation:** migrate the registry value and `GLOBAL_DLQ` to `parking_lot`; preserve catalog/audit behavior and no-await critical sections. Document the per-site invariant audit.
- **Proof:** one panicked task cannot poison `GLOBAL_DLQ` or webhook state and does not degrade another connection's simple or extended protocol operation.

### S3 — Regression gate and exclusions

- **Owned files:** `scripts/check-no-poisoning-locks.sh`, `scripts/check-no-poisoning-locks.test.sh`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, and only required comment locations in `delta_sink.rs` / `iceberg_sink.rs`.
- **Failing tests first:** mutate a temporary copy with an unannotated forbidden lock and assert the checker exits nonzero with its exact `path:line`; mutate/retain the two justified sink occurrences and assert the real tree exits zero with the exact success line.
- **Implementation:** scope the checker to production source in gateway/control/sql/types plus the two explicit connector exceptions; add checker and self-test to CI.
- **Proof:** future poisoning locks cannot enter the audited scope silently, while the narrowly-scoped await-safe exclusions remain visible and verified.
