## 4. Vertical Slices

- **Slice S1: DeltaSink and IcebergSink Async Conversion**
  - *Goal*: Convert `DeltaSink` and `IcebergSink` methods to `async fn`, delete `run_async` and `block_in_place`, and update `SinkConnector` trait & call sites.
  - *Verification*: `async_hygiene_tests.rs::test_delta_and_iceberg_sink_async_no_blocked_workers` (P1).

- **Slice S2: S3 Source Plain Async Polling**
  - *Goal*: Refactor `s3_source.rs::sync_files_from_store` to `async fn` on caller's runtime; remove `std::thread::spawn` and runtime creation.
  - *Verification*: `s3_source_ingestion_tests.rs::test_multi_source_s3_polling_thread_constancy` (P2).

- **Slice S3: Cold GC Async Concurrency-Limited Deletion**
  - *Goal*: Replace `std::thread::sleep` in `cold_gc.rs` with an async, concurrency-limited delete pipeline.
  - *Verification*: `cold_gc_safety_tests.rs::test_cold_gc_async_bounded_concurrency`.

- **Slice S4: Exchange Task Supervision & Shutdown**
  - *Goal*: Replace 13 fire-and-forget `tokio::spawn` calls in `service.rs` and `multiplexer.rs` with `TaskTracker`/`JoinSet` and `CancellationToken`; format errors with `RS-XXXX` codes.
  - *Verification*: `exchange_supervision_tests.rs::test_mid_shuffle_shutdown_task_cleanup` (P3).

- **Slice S5: Configurable Exchange Network Policy & Timeouts**
  - *Goal*: Expose `connect_timeout_ms`, `rpc_timeout_ms`, `max_retries`, and `backoff_jitter_ms` in `rockstream.toml` / `ExchangeConfig`; remove hardcoded 250ms timeout in `pool.rs`.
  - *Verification*: `rockstream_config_tests.rs::test_exchange_config_toml_defaults`.

- **Slice S6: Peer Circuit Breaker & Retry Budget**
  - *Goal*: Implement peer circuit-breaking logic and bounded retries with jittered backoff on all shuffle RPCs.
  - *Verification*: `exchange_circuit_breaker_tests.rs::test_circuit_breaker_on_unreachable_peer` (P4) and `exchange_coordination_sim_tests.rs` with `buggify!()`.

- **Slice S7: Dynamic Frame Channel Sizing & Throughput Benchmark**
  - *Goal*: Size `service.rs` frame channel dynamically from `ExchangeConfig` row budget instead of hardcoded 64.
  - *Verification*: `exchange_service_tests.rs::test_frame_channel_row_budget_sizing` and throughput benchmark in `benches/exchange_bench.rs` (P5).
