- `hot_key_detection_trips_at_fifty_times_median` proves detection at ≥50×.
- `skew_control_loop_waits_thirty_seconds_then_triggers_split` proves the
  controller holds fire at `t=0` and emits a split at
  `SKEW_SPLIT_TRIGGER_WINDOW.as_millis() + 1`.
- `incremental_equals_batch_for_bucketed_hot_key_aggregate` proves bucketed
  aggregate output is bit-identical to the unsplit oracle across randomized
  insert/retract sequences (`PROPTEST_SEED=4701`, 64 cases).
- `hot_key_split_during_concurrent_migration_sim` is the permanent regression
  seed for the concurrent bucket-map bump race
  (`buggify!("hotkey.concurrent_bucket_map_bump", p)`, seed 4704).
