Additionally, two new pipeline-level oracle tests (written during Prove to
close a gap between the plan's Test Commitments table and the Phase-3
implementation) prove end-to-end correctness of the row-budget coupling using
a real `WorkerStreamMultiplexer` ↔ `ShuffleServer` gRPC round trip:

- `quantum_coupled_exchange_rechunking_incremental_equals_batch` — epoch
  payloads are rechunked into `max_rows_per_quantum`-bounded frames (budget=3);
  `FlowController::acquire_credit` genuinely suspends the sender across chunks
  until the receiver's `ShuffleAck` releases row credit. 40 randomized
  INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE epochs; incremental result equals batch recomputation.
- `durable_fallback_after_fast_path_failure_incremental_equals_batch` — peer
  address left unresolvable (induced gRPC failure) forces every epoch through
  the durable object-store fallback (`multiplexer.connection_count() == 0`
  asserted per epoch); per-epoch `catch_up_durable()` replay (idempotent —
  verified no duplicate delivery on a second catch-up pass) is proven equal to
  batch recomputation over 40 randomized epochs.
