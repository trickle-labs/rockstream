## Unresolved gaps

- Current `MemoryCategory` defines eight coarse ledger categories and combines block/metadata caches; the source outcome names seven v0.72 classes plus earlier worker storage allocations, including a local object-store disk cache. The inspected ledger/storage interfaces do not show a one-to-one mapping or an object-store cache ledger metric. The acceptance outcome is settled by the parent contract, but `v072_memory_ownership` must supply the missing allocation-to-owner/category reconciliation and RSS evidence.
