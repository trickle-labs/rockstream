## Notable scope decisions

- Unified `ShutdownCoordinator` supporting OS signal interception (`SIGTERM`, `SIGINT`) and configurable shutdown timeout (`--shutdown-timeout-secs` and `cluster.shutdown_timeout_secs`, default: 30s).
- Self-fencing watchdog timer enforcing hard process termination with fatal diagnostic `RS-3023` if graceful drain exceeds the configured deadline.
- Management HTTP endpoints (`/live`, `/ready`, `/health`) co-located with `/metrics`, supporting structured dependency health checks, replication lag metrics, and actionable `RS-XXXX` diagnostic reasons.
- Gateway connection drain sending PostgreSQL standard notice `57P01` and draining in-flight queries before socket termination.
- Worker graceful drain flushing active SlateDB epochs, releasing shard leases to the control plane for zero-delay takeover by surviving peer workers, and purging exchange connections.
- Control service graceful shutdown persisting shard lease and topology snapshots to shared storage, stepping down Raft leadership cleanly, and emitting structured audit records.
