## Exit Criteria Verification

All Proof criteria in `NEW_ROADMAP.md` (v0.51.25, line 1251) were verified against `.claude/v0.51.25-evidence.md` and the release gates below.

### Proof Claims

- [x] **Claim 1: Zero Blocked-Worker Events**: Sustained Delta & Iceberg sink workload shows zero blocked-worker events. Proven by `crates/rockstream-connectors/tests/async_hygiene_tests.rs::test_delta_and_iceberg_sink_async_no_blocked_workers`.
- [x] **Claim 2: Bounded Thread Creation Under Multi-Source Polling**: Polling 4 S3 sources for 10,000 iterations creates bounded, constant OS threads, not one per poll. Proven by `crates/rockstream-connectors/tests/s3_source_ingestion_tests.rs::test_multi_source_s3_polling_thread_constancy`.
- [x] **Claim 3: Mid-Shuffle Shutdown Supervision**: Mid-shuffle shutdown completes with all tasks joined/cancelled and 0 orphaned tasks observed. Proven by `crates/rockstream-runtime/tests/exchange_task_tracker_tests.rs::test_exchange_service_graceful_shutdown_joins_all_tasks`.
- [x] **Claim 4: Explicit Configurable Network Policy & Circuit Breaker**: Unreachable peer is circuit-broken after retry budget; all timeout/retry parameters settable from `rockstream.toml` with default asserted. Proven by `crates/rockstream-runtime/tests/exchange_circuit_breaker_tests.rs` & `crates/rockstream-cli/tests/rockstream_config_tests.rs`.
- [x] **Claim 5: Frame Channel Dynamic Sizing & Stall Removal**: Resized frame channel removes 64-frame stall observed under high-fan-out shuffle. Proven by `crates/rockstream-runtime/src/exchange/service.rs::test_frame_channel_row_budget_sizing`.
