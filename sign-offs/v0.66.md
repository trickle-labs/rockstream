## Exit Criteria Verification

- [ ] V066-01: Typed reads and real drain, migration, and backup paths are present. Evidence covers control-service migration, Docker-backed drain, and release-process backup. A release-process migration, real cancellation effects, and incompatible-version rejection for every method remain unproved.
- [ ] V066-02: Standalone and multi-worker status transcripts cover topology changes. Request and ACK waiter capacity and fill now have saturation and recovery tests. Headroom, health, lifecycle, and operation-count source changes still lack the full standalone and multi-worker matrix; process health remains explicitly unknown.
- [ ] V066-03: Persisted operation transitions, backup success, drain waiting and retry, and successful drain outcomes are covered. Exact release-process failure and cancellation outcomes remain missing.
- [ ] V066-04: Durable idempotency and backup replay after process restart are tested. Concurrent independent-process retries and changed-payload conflicts after effect and restart remain unproved.
- [ ] V066-05: Store failure, reopen, and successful backup replay are covered. Production-process kills before, during, and after effects still lack reconciliation proof.
- [ ] V066-06: Pre-handoff cancellation and late-cancel rejection are covered. Real executor interruption races and retained-data assertions at irreversible boundaries remain unproved.
- [ ] V066-07: Page, request, operation-history, drain-queue, and per-executor ACK waiter bounds have named limits. Status exposes request and waiter fill and capacity. Integrated waiter saturation, expiry behavior under a frozen resource profile, and queue-age recovery proof remain incomplete.
- [ ] V066-08: Release-process status and backup transcripts exist, and every documented management read command now runs against a release process with exact JSON output checks. Release-process migration and cancellation proof and the full secret-free output matrix remain incomplete.
