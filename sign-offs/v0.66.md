## Exit Criteria Verification

- [ ] V066-01: Typed reads and real drain, migration, and backup executors are present. Docker process tests prove drain, shard movement, and recipient-open acknowledgement; cancellation effects and compatibility coverage for every method remain unproved.
- [ ] V066-02: Standalone and multi-worker status transcripts cover topology and operation-count changes. A complete field-to-owner matrix is missing for headroom, health, lifecycle, and request fill; health remains explicitly unknown.
- [ ] V066-03: Persisted operation transitions, backup success, drain waiting, and successful drain outcomes are covered. Exact release-process failure and cancellation outcomes remain missing.
- [ ] V066-04: Durable idempotency and backup replay after process restart are tested. Concurrent independent-process retries and changed-payload conflicts after effect and restart remain unproved.
- [ ] V066-05: Store failure, reopen, and successful backup replay are covered. Production-process kills before, during, and after effects still lack reconciliation proof.
- [ ] V066-06: Pre-handoff cancellation and late-cancel rejection are covered. Real executor interruption races and retained-data assertions at irreversible boundaries remain unproved.
- [ ] V066-07: Page, request, operation-history, and drain-queue bounds plus expiry cleanup exist. Migration and backup acknowledgement-waiter maps are unbounded; admission saturation and recovery proofs remain missing.
- [ ] V066-08: Release-process status and backup transcripts exist, with Docker-backed drain and shard-movement evidence. The complete executable documentation matrix, cancellation coverage, and secret-free output proof remain incomplete.
