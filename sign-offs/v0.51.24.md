- [x] **Claim 3: `TOPK_BUFFER_LIMIT` Overflow Resolution**: The previously-fatal `TOPK_BUFFER_LIMIT` overflow scenario completes with correct results and a published spill metric instead of `OpError::topk_buffer_overflow`. Proven by `arrangement_spill_correctness_tests::test_topk_spill_buffer_overflow_resolved`.
- [x] **Claim 4: Latency Cost Criterion Benchmark**: A `criterion` benchmark documents the measured latency cost of the spilled path against the in-memory baseline as a new accepted number. Proven by `rtk cargo bench -p rockstream-ops --bench spill_latency -- --test`.
