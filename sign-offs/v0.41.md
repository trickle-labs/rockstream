## Notes

All 14 proof claims for v0.41 are independently verified. The transaction state machine now correctly tracks `'I'`/`'T'`/`'E'` status bytes through full error/rollback lifecycles. Savepoints are implemented as in-memory write-buffer checkpoints (not individually persisted to SlateDB — only COMMIT flushes). LISTEN/NOTIFY is a single-node in-memory registry with transactional delivery semantics (deferred to COMMIT, discarded on ROLLBACK). psycopg3 `connection.notifies()` interoperability verified under the testcontainers feature flag. No items have been deferred to a later version.
