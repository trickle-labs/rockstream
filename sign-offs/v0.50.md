## Notes

Full evidence (commands, outputs, regression seeds, and the exact proof
mapping) is recorded in `.claude/v0.50-evidence.md`. v0.50 delivers SQL
compiler support for recursive CTEs (`WITH RECURSIVE`, monotone semi-naive
fixed-point iteration plus non-monotone full-recompute fallback with a
registered rejection code, distributed cross-shard convergence with
stall/cost-spike safety), lateral joins for nested JSON/arrays with
per-row retraction correctness, and hopping/session windows sharing the
existing tumbling-window state-key/watermark/compaction family — all
proven bit-identical to the DataFusion batch oracle, including Nexmark
q11 sessionization closing the gap noted in `NEW_ROADMAP.md:161`. New
durability coverage spans LFS and MinIO/TestContainers restarts for
recursion state, hop windows, and session windows; new seeded `SimRuntime`
tests (`recursion.inner_frontier_stall`, `recursion.per_shard_cost_spike`,
`recursion.exchange_delta_reorder`) prove distributed recursion safety
under fault injection. Nothing deferred.
