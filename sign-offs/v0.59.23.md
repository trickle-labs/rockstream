## Notable scope decisions

- `CapacityThresholdManifest` and `ProfileThresholds` cryptographically seal measured capacity thresholds, hardware identities, concurrency levels, workload digests, and error ranges with SHA-256 digests.
- `CapacityThresholdStore` provides durable create-only storage across LocalFileSystem (LFS) and MinIO (S3), rejecting mutations to sealed manifests and utilizing explicit scan-and-delete for chunk cleanup without SlateDB range deletions.
- `EXPLAIN INCREMENTAL ESTIMATE` reports canonical arrangement sharing (three maintained arrangements for twenty consumers), selected physical join strategy (classic vs factorized with bounded payload state), and source statistic provenance (`Connector`, `Catalog`, `Fallback`).
- `CapacityReleaseGate` enforces complete signed profile sets (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`), rejecting candidates with mismatched commit SHAs, missing required workloads, underprovisioned hardware, or unobserved metrics.
- Sizing guidance documentation is deterministically regenerable directly from immutable raw measurement chunks, linking each error range to its raw record digest.
