## Notes

Full evidence (commands, outputs, regression seeds, and the exact proof
mapping) is recorded in `.claude/v0.51-evidence.md`. v0.51 delivers WAL
elision for derived intermediate operator shards on the direct-gRPC,
same-host shared-memory, and durable-catch-up fast paths — proven
bit-identical to the DataFusion batch oracle both on the success path and
across LFS/real-MinIO restarts, with no dependence on SlateDB range
deletion — plus tight backpressure coupling between `max_rows_per_quantum`
and network-buffer depth, backed by a named row-in-flight bound, the
`shuffle_rows_in_flight` fill-level metric, and seeded `SimRuntime` fault
injection (`exchange.shm_segment_unavailable`, seed `5101`;
`exchange.domain_rebuild_during_drain`, seed `5102`). Two pipeline-level
oracle tests pre-committed to in the plan's Test Commitments table but found
missing during Prove
(`quantum_coupled_exchange_rechunking_incremental_equals_batch`,
`durable_fallback_after_fast_path_failure_incremental_equals_batch`) were
written during this pass rather than deferred. The optimized fast path
measures ~91% faster than the legacy WAL-on path on the complex-DAG hot-path
benchmark, well over the required >30% throughput increase, and is protected
by a checked-in regression baseline and CI gate. Nothing deferred.
