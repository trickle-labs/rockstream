## Exit Criteria Verification

All Proof criteria in `NEW_ROADMAP.md` (v0.51.15, line 1061) were verified against `.claude/v0.51.15-evidence.md` and the release gates below.

### Proof Claims

- [x] **P1 — Bit-identical crash recovery on LFS and MinIO**: Crash injection at every prepare/commit/ack boundary recovers bit-identically on LFS and MinIO object storage. Proven by `source_durability_lfs_tests` (`crash_before_prepare_recovers_exact_checkpoint`, `crash_after_prepare_before_m3_commit_recovers_exact_checkpoint`, `crash_after_m3_commit_before_ack_recovers_exact_checkpoint`, `crash_after_ack_recovers_exact_checkpoint`) and `source_durability_minio_tests` (matching tests).
- [x] **P2 — Highest committed checkpoint restart**: Recovery starts from the highest committed checkpoint only. Proven by `restart_uses_only_highest_committed_token` on both LFS and MinIO test suites.
- [x] **P3 — SHOW SOURCE STATUS live state without credential leakage**: `SHOW SOURCE STATUS` reports live owner, checkpoint, lag, buffer depth, and blocked state while redacting credentials to `<redacted>`. Proven by `source_ddl_postgres_webhook_tests::show_source_status_reports_exact_live_owner_checkpoint_lag_buffer_and_redacts_credentials`.
- [x] **P4 — Seeded SimRuntime ownership, failover, and single-token epoch guarantee**: One committed token per epoch survives ownership contention, worker failure, and retry. Proven by `source_coordination_sim_tests` (`seeded_competing_owners_commit_one_fenced_token_per_epoch`, `seeded_owner_crash_after_prepare_retries_from_last_committed_token`, `seeded_owner_crash_after_m3_commit_acks_once_after_recovery`).
- [x] **P5 — Webhook delivery committed before ack and exactly-once through failover**: Webhook delivery is committed to M3 before sending HTTP 202 acknowledgement and is exactly once through owner failover. Proven by `source_durability_lfs_tests::webhook_returns_202_only_after_durable_m3_commit`, `source_durability_minio_tests::webhook_returns_202_only_after_durable_m3_commit`, and `source_coordination_sim_tests::seeded_webhook_retry_after_owner_failover_is_exactly_once`.
- [x] **P6 — PAUSE/RESUME/DROP lifecycle cleanup without SlateDB range deletion**: Source lifecycle actions fence owners, preserve or recover committed checkpoints, and clean up source runtime prefixes without relying on SlateDB range deletion. Proven by `source_cleanup_tests` (`pause_fences_owner_and_preserves_recoverable_checkpoint`, `resume_recovers_from_exact_committed_checkpoint`, `drop_scan_deletes_only_named_source_runtime_without_range_delete`) and `source_coordination_sim_tests::seeded_lifecycle_race_never_revives_dropped_or_paused_source`.
