## Notable scope decisions

- `contracts/sql-type-matrix.toml` pins PostgreSQL 18.0 (`postgres:18.0@sha256:41fc5342eefba6cc2ccda736aaf034bbbb7c3df0fdb81516eba1ba33f360162c`) with multi-arch and architecture-specific digests recorded.
- Coarse umbrella capabilities (`language.aggregates`, `language.relational-operators`, `language.analytics-time`) were decomposed into 10 fine-grained operation cells in `capabilities.toml` with honest tiers, reachability, and verified proof targets.
- System limits (`MAX_IN_FLIGHT_ROWS`, `MAX_CONN_MEMORY`, `MAX_CONNECTIONS`, `MAX_PREPARED_STMTS`, `MAX_PORTALS`, `MAX_CURSORS`, `MAX_IDENTIFIER_LEN`, `MAX_DECIMAL_PRECISION`, `MAX_VIEW_DAG_DEPTH`) are consolidated into authoritative metadata in `rockstream-types::limits` and generate `docs/limits.md`.
- `rockstream_binary_v1` is enforced as the deterministic collation standard; unsupported locale-dependent collations fail closed with `RS-1013`, and floating-point equi-joins fail closed with `RS-1019`.
