## Proof Claims Verification

- [x] **`formal/m4_self_fencing.fizz` exhaustive (BFS) verification terminates
  at CI-fast bounds**
  - **Evidence**: systematically isolating each bound one at a time (see
    `formal/findings.md` "Post-v0.42.3 Remediation Results") found
    `MAX_OUTAGES` — not worker/shard count as the v0.42.1 notes speculated —
    is the dominant driver of the state-space explosion (`MAX_OUTAGES=2` was
    ~16× the node count of `MAX_OUTAGES=1` at otherwise-equal-or-larger
    bounds). Lowered `MAX_OUTAGES` from 2 to 1 in
    `formal/m4_self_fencing.fizz`, keeping every other bound at its
    originally committed value (`NUM_WORKERS=3, NUM_SHARDS=2,
    SELF_FENCE_AFTER=3, MAX_EPOCH=2, MAX_CHECKPOINT=2`). No
    symmetry-reduction feature was needed.
  - **Command**: `fizz formal/m4_self_fencing.fizz`
  - **Result**: 31,456 nodes explored, 12,946 valid, 1,966 unique states,
    ~5.4s exploration + ~0.25s liveness check.

- [x] **`M4_L1_RecoveryProgress` and `M4_L2_NoPermanentBlock` are positively
  confirmed (not just failed-to-disprove) under exhaustive BFS**
  - **Evidence**: at `MAX_OUTAGES=1`, exhaustive BFS first genuinely failed
    `M4_L1_RecoveryProgress` with a reproducible counterexample:
    `GrantLease` could re-grant a shard's lease back to a worker already in
    `cp.dead_workers` because its guard only checked `w.status == "active"`
    (true until self-fence), letting an adversarial-but-FizzBee-"fair"
    schedule that always re-elects the same dead worker starve every other
    worker forever — a real liveness bug, not a state-space artifact. Fixed
    by adding `require worker_id not in cp.dead_workers` to `GrantLease`
    (`formal/m4_self_fencing.fizz`). Re-run after the fix: `M4-S1`–`M4-S4`
    (safety), `COV_M4`, `M4_L1_RecoveryProgress`, and `M4_L2_NoPermanentBlock`
    all hold.
  - **Command**: `fizz formal/m4_self_fencing.fizz`

- [x] **M4's `continue-on-error: true` is removed; all five specs
  (smoke, M1, M2, M3, M4) are one hard-gate step, completing the v0.18

  - **Evidence**: `.github/workflows/ci.yml`'s `formal-verify` job's

  - **Command**: inspect `.github/workflows/ci.yml`

## Notes

`NUM_SHARDS=3` was tried during isolation testing and found to crash
(unrelated to tractability): `ControlPlane.Init` and `Worker.Init` hardcode
2-element literal lists (`self.leases = [-1, -1]`, etc.) instead of building
them from `NUM_SHARDS`. Out of scope for v0.42.3; any future shard-count
change to this model requires a spec generalization first.

This closes Phase 11.5 (v0.42.1–v0.42.3) and the v0.18 binding contract for
all four coordination-protocol FizzBee models (M1–M4), unblocking Phase 12
(v0.43), which requires a working `make verify` gate for its new M5 model.
