## Exit Criteria Verification

All Proof criteria in `NEW_ROADMAP.md` (v0.52) were verified against `.claude/v0.52-evidence.md` and the release gates below.

### Proof Claims

- [x] **Claim 1: Real Kafka & Postgres CDC Decode Failure Quarantine**: Malformed record in real Kafka & Postgres CDC stream durably queryable in `rockstream_catalog.dead_letter_queue` while offset advances. Proven by `crates/rockstream-connectors/tests/dlq_connector_tests.rs::<test_postgres_cdc_decode_failure_quarantines_to_dlq>`.
- [x] **Claim 2: Prepare/Commit Crash Recovery**: Crash recovery at prepare/commit boundary recovers with zero lost quarantined rows and zero re-applied offsets. Proven by `crates/rockstream-sim/tests/dlq_crash_recovery_sim_tests.rs::<test_dlq_crash_recovery_deterministic>`.
- [x] **Claim 3: ALTER SOURCE REPLAY**: `ALTER SOURCE ... REPLAY DEAD_LETTER_QUEUE SINCE .. UNTIL ..` re-ingests exact records and increments `replay_attempt` correctly across repeated replays. Proven by `crates/rockstream-gateway/tests/dlq_replay_command_tests.rs::<test_alter_source_replay_and_dismiss_dlq>`.
- [x] **Claim 4: ALTER SOURCE DISMISS**: `ALTER SOURCE ... DISMISS DEAD_LETTER_QUEUE WHERE ...` removes exactly matching rows and nothing else. Proven by `crates/rockstream-gateway/tests/dlq_replay_command_tests.rs::<test_alter_source_replay_and_dismiss_dlq>`.
- [x] **Claim 5: Threshold Warning & State Degradation**: Exceeding `dlq_warn_threshold` emits `RS-1004` as `NOTICE` and metric; continuous quarantining reaches `BLOCKED` state. Proven by `crates/rockstream-connectors/tests/dlq_backpressure_threshold_tests.rs::<test_dlq_warn_threshold_and_blocked_degradation>`.
- [x] **Claim 6: Bounded Retention GC**: Source quarantining past `DLQ_RETENTION` shows oldest rows expired and table's on-disk size flat. Proven by `crates/rockstream-connectors/tests/dlq_retention_tests.rs::<test_dlq_retention_gc_purges_expired_entries>`.
